Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my uploaded document photos be uploaded to any server? ▾
No. All operations, including JPEG compression, rotation, cropping, and PDF compilation, take place locally inside your browser's JavaScript memory. No data is transmitted to any cloud server.
Why does the receiver have trouble downloading images from standard PDFs? ▾
PDF viewers do not support right-click saving of embedded raster graphics. WorkPDF provides both universal clean JPEG streams in PDF and a dedicated ZIP download option so receivers can extract original images directly.
How does the target size (MB/KB) compression work? ▾
The engine runs a binary-search algorithm over canvas resolution and JPEG quality factors until the rendered image binary falls strictly under your specified target limit (e.g. 1024 KB or 500 KB).
